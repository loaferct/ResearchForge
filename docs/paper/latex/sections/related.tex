\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{AI-scientist systems and their audits.} End-to-end systems generate ideas, run experiments and write papers \citep{lu2024the,yamada2025the,schmidgall2025agent,schmidgall2025agentrxiv,gottweis2025accelerating,mitchener2025kosmos,miyai2025jr,li2026autosota,wang2026sibylautoresearch,zheng2026rethinking}. Several keep a persistent structured state (Kosmos's ``structured world model'' \citep{mitchener2025kosmos}; a ``persistent world state'' in \citep{weidener2026rethinking}) and trace statements to sources, as our harness does. Independent audits report weak novelty assessment \citep{beel2025evaluating}, hypotheses that fail against null models \citep{nusrat2025when}, and a verification gap between what systems claim and what reviewers can check \citep{ding2026autonomous}. Process-level evaluations find that agents do not revise conclusions against their own evidence \citep{fei2026how}, reach right answers through wrong mechanisms \citep{eulig2026position}, and differ in feedback control across runs \citep{li2026beyond}; ARAC scores research-process alignment \citep{cui2026arac}.

\paragraph{Novelty and idea assessment.} Benchmarks of novelty judgement use human labels: RINoBench \citep{schopf2026is}, NovBench \citep{wu2026novbench}, and NovGauge, which finds that over 70\% of correct positive judgements cite evidence that does not support them \citep{zhang2026novgauge}. LLM judges show a ``novelty mirage'' \citep{sinhahajari2026on}, a medium-novelty bias \citep{schopf2026thinkproberespond} and style sensitivity \citep{ji2026style}; they fall short of expert preferences \citep{chen2026ideation}. Assessment systems retrieve and compare \citep{shahid2025literaturegrounded,moussa2025scholareval,qiao2026innoeval,zhang2026opennovelty,hou2026memonoveltyagent,shen2026navigating,han2026noveltyaware,yan2025noveltyrank}; ResearchStudio-Idea includes a prior-art collision check for its own generated ideas \citep{zhao2026researchstudioidea}. Idea generators iterate against literature \citep{wang2023scimon,baek2024researchagent,radensky2024humanllm,li2024chain,ke2025biodisco}; human studies show that ideas judged novel lose their advantage after execution \citep{si2024can,si2025the}. Lit2Test asks proposals to precommit a falsifying outcome \citep{wang2026what}.

\paragraph{Temporal evaluation.} ForeSci hides post-cutoff papers to evaluate forward-looking research judgement and finds ``evidence-decision decoupling'' \citep{tian2026foresci}; Reconstruction recovers a paper's idea from its pre-publication bibliography under an anti-leakage protocol \citep{chen2026reconstruction}; AgentIdeaBench compares static and active exploration for ideation \citep{mo2026agentideabench}. Search-time contamination can inflate agent benchmark scores \citep{wang2026searchtime}. TPAD differs in its target: not recovering or forecasting an idea, but recognising that a given idea is already realised, with the realising paper as checkable evidence.

\paragraph{Falsification and belief revision.} POPPER validates hypotheses by sequential falsification \emph{experiments} with error control \citep{huang2025automated}; a position paper argues for falsification-first agentic science \citep{fa2026sound}. LLMs show confirmation bias that prompting partly reduces \citep{jhaveri2026failing}, and they fail to retract conclusions after weakening evidence \citep{sadhu2026derelab}. Our intervention is literature-level: the agent searches for published work that contradicts its own conclusions.

\paragraph{Evidence and source integrity.} Cited synthesis \citep{skarlinski2024language,asai2024openscholar} and citation evaluation \citep{gao2023enabling} are established; citation URLs and BibTeX entries are frequently wrong \citep{rao2026detecting,rao2026bibtex,reizinger2026hallmark}, errors originate at orchestrators \citep{hirsch2026who}, and claim-level auditability has been proposed as a design target \citep{rasheed2026from}. Upstream, 12\% of sampled OpenAlex abstracts have integrity issues \citep{kim2026one}, and one injected misleading document raises deep-research agents' false-conclusion adoption from 0\% to 54.7\% \citep{zhu2026is}. EviBound gates experiment claims on machine-checkable artefacts \citep{chen2025evidencebound}.

\paragraph{Search control.} Deep-research agents stagnate and stop poorly \citep{soudani2026when,zhang2026to,liu2026diagnosing}; evidence-sufficiency criteria reduce premature stopping \citep{choubey2026dont}. Benchmarks target reports and QA \citep{du2025deepresearch,zhong2026draco,xie2026dreval} or literature discovery \citep{xiong2026autoresearchbench}.
