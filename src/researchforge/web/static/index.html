Check your research idea against the literature.

ResearchForge finds the closest existing work, argues against the idea, and shows the evidence behind every conclusion.

One or two sentences. A question works best.

A model saying an idea is novel proves nothing.

ResearchForge reports only what it can point to, and marks what kind of statement you are reading. The rule in the margin carries that meaning everywhere.

  • EvidenceA verbatim quote from a retrieved paper, checked against its text.
  • InferenceReasoning drawn from cited evidence.
  • HypothesisProposed, not yet tested. Gaps and modifications live here.
  • Experimental resultObserved in an experiment you approved.

How an investigation runs

Each phase must record its findings before the next starts. A phase that cannot is marked incomplete, never padded with text.

  1. FormalizeTurn the idea into a testable question.
  2. SearchQuery arXiv, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and Crossref.
  3. AnalyzeRead the closest papers and check their code.
  4. Map the fieldGroup the approaches and place your idea.
  5. Argue against itFind the closest work and the strongest objection.
  6. Find gapsKeep only gaps backed by quoted passages.
  7. Propose changesTwo to five stronger variations.
  8. Design the testBaselines, metrics, and the result that would disprove it.

What you get

A verdict with its reasons

One of four assessments, with the rationale and the papers closest to your idea.

The case against your idea

The strongest objection, the most dangerous confounder, and the question that most needs an answer.

Gaps you can check

Every gap lists the passages behind it and what is still unverified.

Directions and a test plan

Two to five modifications with honest difficulty ratings, and an experiment plan that states what result would falsify the hypothesis.

Implementation difficulty moderate, because it needs a per-request budget predictor.

An evidence trail you can reopen

Every search, source, claim and tool call is saved in a project folder you can audit or resume.

research_projects/kv-cache-eviction/
  papers/            retrieved papers, with sources
  evidence/claims/   claims and checked quotes
  hypotheses/        landscape, critique, gaps
  experiments/       plans and approved runs
  reports/           report.md, report.html

A report no model wrote

Fifteen sections assembled from recorded data, so it cannot cite a paper that was never retrieved.
